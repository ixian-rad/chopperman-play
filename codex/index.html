    Aim at the center and align the blade with the long grain guide. Tap Space or the swing button to start the timing meter; tap again in the green zone. For direct control, aim on the log, pull the mouse down, and release. Longer, quicker pulls add power. The pull line previews the exact power and grain alignment; sideways pulls turn the blade. Mouse blows do not depend on the timing meter.

    Yard mode: WASD walks, Shift runs, and right drag looks. Click / Space chops a highlighted tree. Each felled tree earns 35 XP and 20 coins. Approach the block and press E (or click it) to split logs. Esc leaves the block. Switch backyards, costume, and axes in Your Backyard. Golden axe is a permanent 180 coin unlock in the Tool Shed. Campaign upgrades also improve your chosen tool; challenge contracts retain their fresh standard stats. V toggles the side panels for an open view; prompts and controls still work.

    Six chapters lead from pine to ironwood. Every third log is an oversized boss with a bonus bounty. A weak chop leaves a particular crack: follow its grain line to deepen it. Once a cut is open, aim near its dark kerf: a gold ring marks the selected wedge point. Press W to drive it using 12 stamina. A seated wedge stays in that cut even if you aim elsewhere; X pulls it for a small friction/depth-based stamina cost. Then aim at a different crack to move it. Wedges need no sharp edge; axe splits can earn clean combos. Splitting wood restores stamina.

    The grain reader is an enlarged, interactive end-grain view: aim and drag there just as on the log. Twisted elm has changing grain. Steel on knotty oak chips your edge by 18%; embedded stone on late ironwood chips it by 12%. The orange aim ring warns when any part of the edge overlaps an inclusion. The Iron Crown has three separate stone-bound sections, divided by faint seams. Work beside each stone and finish that section before moving to another; released pockets leave real gaps. All three must fall for the boss bounty. Repair in the shed for 12 coins. Rain soaks wood over time, raising resistance and lowering friction. Snow accumulates frost; centered frozen wood gets a brittle bonus. Conditions persist as weather changes and snapshot when you swing. Forked elm has two leaning limbs and a lower interlocked crotch: remove both limbs first, then open the crotch. C finds the next section; each has its own grain and persistent cuts. A driven wedge stays seated until that section breaks. Heart-rotted hickory has a dark, soft core: a light blow crumbles it, but the dense outer walls still need separate cuts. Wind pushes loose pieces and light chips more than heavy billets; the yard ribbon shows gusts. Hollow oak has a real cavity: strike each standing wall and align radially. C aims at the next wall. Impact effects respect reduced-motion preferences and can be toggled in field settings.

    Open Campaign / Challenge contracts for a 90 second seeded sprint, the daily six-log route, or Precision Six. Precision gives twelve strikes to clear six mint-ring targets: match the marked point, grain, and requested power. Each log pays its average strike quality, plus first-strike and bullseye streak bonuses; misses lower that average, and extra blows lose first-strike bonuses. Resetting does not restore attempts. All use a fresh axe and separate local records. The timer begins with your first swing; slow motion and upgrades are disabled during contracts. A result card awards bronze, silver, or gold and tracks your last five runs. Equal scores rank by time, misses, then swings. Replay or return to your saved campaign anytime between swings.
