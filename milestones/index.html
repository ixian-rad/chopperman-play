Costs add up every game-build run of that harness that started before the tag, from the same run log as the build log; play-site design runs and the channel-art kit are left out. Reported and token-priced figures are list-price equivalents, not invoices. Gemini figures are est.: measured tokens × the Gemini 4 Argon list price, login-based and not billed. “Text under 16 px” is measured from the game’s own HUD and panels in that frame (text drawn inside the 3D canvas can’t be measured). The games’ legibility rule is text at least 16 px and HUD numbers at least 20 px (the legibility pass came at Claude iter-3, Codex iter-10 and DeepSeek iter-27). The flag reports what was measured in that frame, including small caps labels that some later builds still have.

What is this?

Chopperman is an experiment comparing four AI coding harnesses: Codex CLI, Claude Code, the DeepSeek Harness and Gemini 4 Argon. Each got the same brief, build a backyard axe-chopping game for the browser, then the same follow-up prompts in the same order. Their games live side by side here, with their milestones, scores, reviews and build costs. Gemini 4 Argon joined later and is still working through the prompts, so it is marked in progress wherever it appears.

The games need WebGL 2. A desktop browser with a mouse is recommended. Click inside a game to start its audio. Classic (pre-assets) builds: Codex · Claude · DeepSeek.

Reviews by the Grain & Gravity channel (“Axe sims, split by the numbers.”). Logo, illustrations and site design by Claude. Screenshots are captures of the games themselves. The key art of Sergey is AI-generated (GPT) illustration.
