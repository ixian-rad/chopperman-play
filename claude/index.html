How to split firewood

Controls

  • Move the mouse over the log top to place the strike point (reticle). The white line is the split plane.
  • Hold left button to charge the meter; drag left/right while holding to turn the blade; release to swing.
  • Or click once to start the meter and click again (or Space) to stop it.
  • Right-drag orbits the camera, Shift+right-drag pans, wheel zooms, Shift+wheel turns the blade.
  • Keyboard: Space start/stop meter · arrows nudge aim · Q/E turn blade · R reset log · B shop · O modes · G sharpen · T time · N weather · K quality · C camera · F physics · M music on/off (volumes and SFX toggle: bottom-left panel) · X quick swing

Scoring

  • Bullseye (within ~7% of the radius from the pith) ×1.5, Clean ×1.2, Off-centre ×0.8, Slab ×0.5.
  • Follow a radial seasoning check (dark crack in the end grain) for −28% split energy and +30% points.
  • Knots (dark ovals) need up to 2× the energy; aim the split plane away from them for a bonus.
  • A rusty nail head on the bark means steel inside: keep the blade off that radial line or chip your edge. A grey stone in the bark is worse.
  • Variants: ◉ knotty, ↻ twisted (+30% energy), Y forked (turn the blade along the branch, through the pith), ☁ punky heart (soft, crumbly, low value), 💧 waterlogged, ❄ ice-glazed (the glaze deflects light blows), ◆ stone, ⬤ huge (several strikes on one line).
  • Boss logs (☠) are huge multi-variant rounds with a toughness bar; every stuck strike on the same line wears it down.
  • The sweet zone on the meter gives tight accuracy; overswinging past it scatters the blow.
  • Consecutive one-hit clean splits build a combo (+15% each). Glances, misses, nails and stuck axes break it.
  • Rain, storm, snow, frost, wind, fog and night all add a score bonus — and make it harder.

Weather & physics

  • Rain: wet bark glances more, wet wood needs ~18% more energy, wet handles slip (gloves help), puddles form.
  • Snow / frost: frozen wood is brittle (−30% energy, more pieces) but icy bark and footing hurt accuracy.
  • Wind: gusts sway your aim and blow chips around. Fog: you can't read the energy needed.
  • Stamina drains per swing (heavier axes cost more) and regenerates while resting; tired swings are weak and wobbly.
